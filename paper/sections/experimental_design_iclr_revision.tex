\section{Experimental Design}
\label{sec:experiments}

\subsection{Data and Temporal Roles}

We use ICEWS14 as a controlled event-forecasting case study. After deterministic
normalization, it contains 7,128 entities, 230 relations, 90,730 facts, and 365
daily timestamps. The first 219 timestamps train the scorer, timestamps
220--292 supply disjoint validation and calibration roles, and timestamps
293--365 form the prequential test stream. The corresponding fact counts are
52,993, 18,439, and 19,298. The entity vocabulary is closed and fixed before
testing. Training facts are deleted at rates
\(\rho\in\{0,0.1,0.2,0.3\}\) with the entity-coverage constraint defined in
Section~\ref{sec:method}; development, calibration, and test facts are unchanged.

\subsection{Scorer, Comparisons, and Metrics}

The frozen scorer is continuous-time DistMult. Frequency and history-based
Repeat provide ranking references. The calibration comparison uses the same
all-entity score tensors for Static, Rolling-1000, Fixed Weighted-1000, and the
exploratory half-life selector. Rank, APS, and validation-selected RAPS rolling
rules are evaluated separately as shortlist operating points.

Filtered MRR and Hits@1/3/10 measure ranking. Reliability is evaluated with
observed-label coverage, positive undercoverage
\((0.90-\widehat{\cov})_+\), full-set query coverage, and partial answer recall.
Utility is measured by mean, median, and P90 set size and by the fraction of
sets equal to the 7,128-entity vocabulary. A relation-side audit retains groups
with at least 250 observed labels across all seeds and representation in every
seed. This support filter defines a diagnostic subset; it does not provide a
conditional-coverage guarantee.

\subsection{Confirmatory Protocol and Inference}

The primary setting is 30\% training-history deletion and target coverage 0.90.
Its estimand is
\[
\begin{aligned}
  \Delta_{\mathrm{roll}}
  &=\frac{1}{|\mathcal{S}|\,|\mathcal{T}|}
    \sum_{s\in\mathcal{S}}\sum_{t\in\mathcal{T}} d_{s,t},\\
  d_{s,t}
  &=(0.90-\widehat{\cov}^{\,s,t}_{\mathrm{Static}})_+
   -(0.90-\widehat{\cov}^{\,s,t}_{\mathrm{Rolling}})_+ .
\end{aligned}
\]
It measures the reduction in timestamp-macro positive undercoverage from
Static to Rolling-1000. The scorer uses five seeds
\(\{17,29,43,59,71\}\). We report a paired seed-resampled circular
moving-block bootstrap with 20,000 replicates and a seven-timestamp block
length; each replicate uses the same timestamp blocks for both methods. Block
lengths 3, 14, and 21 provide a sensitivity check. The code, resolved
configurations, preprocessing steps, and derived paper tables are available at
\url{https://github.com/xywang815/riskcal-tkg-w}.
